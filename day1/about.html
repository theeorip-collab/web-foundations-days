About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for capturing ideas and keeping your notes organised. It helps you quickly write down important thoughts before you forget them.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea into the note field.
  2. Click the Add Note button.
  3. Review your saved notes in the notes list.

Features

  • Quickly capture new ideas.
  • Keep your notes organised.
  • View all your notes in one place.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add a new note
Esc Clear the note field
